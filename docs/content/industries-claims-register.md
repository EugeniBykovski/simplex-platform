# Industries: claims register

Every potentially consequential claim on `/industries` and its six detail pages, for SF-147. Source: `Industries.dc.html` and `IndustryPage.dc.html`, design version `1793953114-553b`. Claude Design was not modified.

**Nothing here is verified.** No founder-approved evidence for any certification, client, delivery or capability exists in the design or the repository. All seven routes are `noindex`, set by the pages, until the decisions below are made.

## Status key

| Status | Meaning | How it is published |
|---|---|---|
| VERIFIED | Backed by founder-approved material | Nothing qualifies yet |
| PROVISIONAL | The design's wording, stating an approach or a capability | Published as designed |
| UNVERIFIED | Worded as fact or as an absolute in the source | Softened |
| DEFERRED | Cannot be published | Removed or replaced |
| CONTEXT | A statement about the sector, not about SimpleX | Published as designed |
